MealSnap Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026
App: MealSnap — Snap a meal, see calories, carbs, protein and fat at a glance.
Developer contact: mealsnap2026@gmail.com

MealSnap is made to help people with diabetes and pre-diabetes, ages 13 and up, keep track of what they eat, one meal at a time. This policy explains what data MealSnap collects, why, and how you can delete it. It is written to match the Data safety section on Google Play. If the app changes, this policy will be updated.

No account, no sign-in

MealSnap does not require you to sign in, and it does not let you create an account in the app. There is no Google Sign-In, social login, or email login in the app. All features work without an account.

Data we collect

MealSnap collects only the data below, and only when you choose to enter it:

  • Other personal info — age and sex. Only if you use the optional My goals calculator. Age and sex are used to suggest daily calories, carbs, protein, and fat. You can skip the calculator entirely and set your goals manually instead, so this data is optional.
  • Health info — meal nutrition, height, and weight. Meal entries (foods, calories, carbohydrates, protein, fat, fiber, and sugar) and, if you use the calculator, your height and weight. Meal data is required for the app to track your day; without a meal entry there is nothing to track.
  • Fitness info — activity level. Only if you use the optional My goals calculator (for example, sedentary or lightly active). This tailors the calorie suggestion to you. It is optional.
  • Photos — meal photos (optional). Only if you choose to attach a photo to a meal, using your camera or photo library through the system photo picker. The photo is shrunk on your phone before saving and is stored only in the app's storage on your phone. A meal's photo is deleted when you delete that meal, and all photos are deleted by the "Delete all my data" button. Optional AI photo calculation: if you tap "✨ Calculate from photo" for a meal, that one meal photo is sent from the app to MealSnap's calculation service, which forwards it to Google Gemini so it can estimate the meal's name, calories, carbohydrates, protein, fat, fiber, and sugar. This happens only when you tap Calculate — photos are never sent in the background or for any other purpose. The app contains no API key and you never provide one. The calculation service stores nothing: it does not save or log the photo; the photo passes through for that one estimate and is then gone. MealSnap itself stores nothing off your phone: your meals, goals, and photos stay in the app's storage on your device. The estimated numbers are filled in for you to check and edit before saving, like all numbers in MealSnap — they are estimates, not medical-grade measurements. Google's handling of the photo forwarded for an estimate is covered by Google's own terms and privacy policy for Gemini API use.

MealSnap does not collect your name, email address, phone number, home address, location, financial information, messages, contacts, calendar, or sexual orientation. It does not collect data about children under 13, because the app is intended for ages 13 and up.

How we use your data

  • App functionality: to save your meals, show your daily totals against your goals, and calculate goal suggestions.
  • Personalization: to tailor the suggested calories, carbs, protein, and fat to you, based on the age, sex, height, weight, and activity level you enter in the calculator.

Almost all of this happens on your phone only. Everything you enter is stored in the app's storage on your device (local storage). MealSnap has no account system, and your entries are never sent to us to keep. The single exception is the optional AI photo calculation described above: when you tap "✨ Calculate from photo", that one meal photo passes through MealSnap's calculation service to Google Gemini for that one estimate. The service stores nothing.

MealSnap does not use your data for advertising or marketing, analytics about how you use the app, developer communications, fraud prevention, or account management.

Data sharing

MealSnap shares nothing on its own — no data is kept by the developer, sent to advertisers, or sent to any other third party, and MealSnap does not sell your data. The only transfer happens at your direction: if you use the optional AI photo calculation, the meal photo you choose passes through MealSnap's calculation service to Google Gemini for that one estimate, as described above; the service stores nothing. Meal entries, goals, and calculator entries are never shared.

Security

Your meals, goals, and calculator entries never leave your phone: MealSnap makes no network connection to send them anywhere. The one exception is the optional AI photo calculation, where the photo you choose travels over a secure HTTPS connection to MealSnap's calculation service, which forwards it over HTTPS to Google Gemini when you tap Calculate — the same kind of secure connection the app's own program files are loaded over.

Data retention and deletion

  • Meals, goals and any meal photos you attach are saved on your phone so you can see your history and progress.
  • You can delete an individual meal inside the app at any time; its photo is deleted with it.
  • You can erase everything at once with the app's "Delete all my data" button, or by clearing the app's/site's data in your phone's settings. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, deleting it on your phone deletes it completely — there is no copy on a server to ask us to remove.

Children

MealSnap is intended for ages 13 and up. It is not intended for children under 13. Teens should use MealSnap with a parent or guardian, and everyone should check their calorie and carbohydrate goals with a doctor.

Health information only

MealSnap is for information only. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition, including diabetes, and it does not tell you what to eat. Please check your calorie and carbohydrate goals with your doctor.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date, and the change will be reflected in the Google Play listing.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about deleting your data: email mealsnap2026@gmail.com.
